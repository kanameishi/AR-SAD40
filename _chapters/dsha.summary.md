The deterministic assessment postulates, for each tectonic source capable of controlling the site demand, its maximum credible earthquake (MCE): the largest reasonably conceivable earthquake under the presently known tectonic framework [@ICOLD2016]. Each source is reported as its own scenario spectrum, preserving the physical identity of the governing earthquake, and the deterministic design spectrum is the envelope of the scenario spectra — the MCE from their means and the MCE (84%) from their 84th percentiles. These spectra are compared with the uniform hazard spectra of the probabilistic assessment.
