The Newmark permanent displacement $D_n$ quantifies the deformation an earthquake accumulates in the sliding mass and is the performance measure from which the seismic coefficients are derived. For the probabilistic spectrum of each evaluated return period and for the MCE, $D_n$ was estimated probabilistically with a set of five published empirical formulations, rigid-block and flexible-block, evaluated with the demand at the site condition of each geometry–material combination. The product is the distribution of $D_n$ by geometry, material and demand that supports the coefficient calculation.
