The model provides individual realizations for AM88, JB07, SR08, BT07, and a fifth formulation selected by the tectonic context: BM17 for projects with subduction control and BM19 for projects with crustal control. The overall design set, identified as `ensemble`, combines AM88, JB07, SR08, BT07, and the active BM branch with equal weights; the rigid family combines AM88, JB07, and SR08, and the flexible family combines BT07 and the active BM branch. For each $k_y$, the displacements of a family are summarized from the weighted empirical distribution of the realizations of its models. All models receive the same joint sample of PGA and spectral accelerations, and a single realization of $\epsilon$ is shared across the models and across all values of $k_y$; each formulation scales that residual through its own $\sigma_{\ln D}$. The sampled PGA is retained with each realization so that the inversion keeps its demand state. The realizations of $D_n$ are calculated for each material class with its mean dynamic properties. The fractiles of the stiffness profiles and of $T_s$ are reported in the dynamic-properties chapter but are not propagated as additional realizations in the displacement calculation. Each material class is kept as an independent input through the inversion to $k_{\max}$; the displacement curves are not averaged across materials before that operation. For each model and realization, the expression [-@eq-kmax-criterion] is projected onto a non-increasing $D_n (k_y)$ curve and inverted in the $(\ln D_n,\ln k_y)$ plane; when $D_a$ falls outside the calculated support, the relation is extended in that plane from the two points at the corresponding end. The inversion is performed first per model within each realization. The root distributions of the models are then combined with the family weights and summarized through the arithmetic mean and the empirical fractiles $q=0.05$, 0.10, 0.16, 0.50, 0.84, 0.90, and 0.95 [@VerriKozlowski2026Newmark]. Because of the decreasing relation between $D_n$ and $k_y$, the fractile associated with the exceedance target satisfies expression [-@eq-kmax-fractile] where $q=0.84$ corresponds to an exceedance probability of $p_e=16%$.

$$
q=1-p_e
$${#eq-kmax-fractile}

The arithmetic mean summarizes the center of the simulated distribution and does not represent a target probability; the $q$ fractiles are the statistics associated with the criterion expressed through $p_e$. The evaluation uses `r .fmti(NS)` realizations for each demand — the probabilistic spectrum of each return period $T_R$ = `r paste(prettyNum(round(sort(TR.gmdp), 0), big.mark = ","), collapse = ", ")` years and the MCE — and a grid of allowable displacements $D_a$ ranging from `r paste(format(range(Da.gmdp), trim = TRUE, scientific = FALSE), collapse = " to ")` cm. This grid contains, for every slope height, the damage thresholds $D_a/H$ = `r paste(.fmt(DAMAGE.CUTS, 1), collapse = ", ")`% of Chapter [-@sec-criteria]. Values of $D_a$ that exceed the calculated support of a realization are obtained through the logarithmic extension defined above.
