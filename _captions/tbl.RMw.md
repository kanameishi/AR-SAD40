Seismic hazard deaggregation summary. Each row corresponds to a return period $T_R$, in years, and each column group to an oscillator period $T_n$, in seconds, with $T_n=0$ corresponding to $\mathrm{PGA}$. Within each group, $M_w$ is the moment magnitude of the magnitude–distance block contributing most to exceedance, dimensionless, and $R$ is the source-to-site distance of that same block, in km; both are the coordinates of the block centre on the same block grid used by the deaggregation figures. Site `%s`.
