Mean MCE and MDE design spectra (PSHA/DSHA) at the reference rock condition $V_{S30} = %.0f$ m/s. The MCE is derived from the deterministic scenarios; the MDE is the mean rock UHS for $T_R = 10{,}000$ yr. The ordinate is the spectral acceleration $S_a(T_n)$, in $g$, with 5%% critical damping; the abscissa is the oscillator period $T_n$, in seconds, with the $T_n = 0$ ordinate labelled $\mathrm{PGA}$. Both axes are logarithmic. Only the mean spectra are shown, without quantile curves or an uncertainty band. Site `%s`.
