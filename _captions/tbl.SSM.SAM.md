Summary inventory of the adopted SAM implementation, grouped by tectonic region type (TRT) and component. `TRT` uses ASC for Active Shallow Crust, SCC for Stable Continental Crust, SIF for Subduction Interface, and SIS for Intraslab and Deep Seismicity. `Comp.` identifies C1, fault sources in active shallow crust; C2, distributed seismicity in active shallow crust; C3, fault sources in stable continental crust; C4, distributed seismicity in stable continental crust; C5, subduction interface; and C6, intraslab and deep seismicity. Column $N$ gives the number of sources in the inventory for each combination; the total of 549 corresponds to the complete local model. `MFD` identifies MFD1, a histogram of incremental annual occurrence rates in evenly spaced magnitude intervals (`EvenlyDiscretizedMFD`); MFD2, a sequence of MFDs of the same type associated with the nodes of a multi-point source (`MultiMFD`); MFD3, a truncated Gutenberg–Richter distribution (`TruncatedGRMFD`); MFD4, an explicit set of nonparametric ruptures with an occurrence-probability distribution associated with each rupture; and MFD5, a combination of seven MFD1 sources and two MFD3 sources at the subduction interface. Columns $M_w$ and $z$ give the minimum–maximum ranges of moment magnitude and depth, respectively, with depth in kilometers. For C1–C4, $z$ reports the extrema of the upper and lower seismogenic depths; for C6, it reports the extrema of hypocentral depth from the sample of up to 200 ruptures per source retained in the inventory. \* For C5, $z$ was obtained from the three-dimensional nodes of the nine interface geometries.
