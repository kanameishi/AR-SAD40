When the report covers more than one site, a leading `siteID` column identifies the site. $T_R$ is the return period, in years, and $P_T$ is the probability of at least one exceedance during the exposure interval of the calculation, as a percentage. The remaining columns are the demand statistics: those headed by a probability level $p$ hold the quantile $Q(p)$, and the one headed `mean` holds the mean, both in the units stated. Site `%s`.
