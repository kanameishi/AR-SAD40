Maximum credible earthquake (MCE) scenario peak ground acceleration, $\mathrm{PGA}$ [%s], at the reference rock condition $V_{S30} = 760$ m/s. Each row is a deterministic scenario $e$, identified in the first column, and $T_n$ is the oscillator period, in seconds. The remaining columns are the probability levels $p$ = %s of the scenario total-variability mixture: those headed by a numerical value hold the conditional quantile $Q(p\mid e)$, and the one headed `mean` holds the conditional mean $E[S_a\mid e]$, both in the units stated.
