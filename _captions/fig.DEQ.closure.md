Design earthquakes by consequence class: each series is the design demand spectrum of a class, obtained as the mean of the two uniform hazard spectra (UHS) whose return periods $T_R$ bound the standard's criterion, and is identified by the stage and the consequence class. The ordinates are maximum horizontal spectral accelerations $S_a(T_n)$, in $g$, with 5%% critical damping; the abscissa is the oscillator period $T_n$, in seconds, whose value $T_n=0$ is labelled $\mathrm{PGA}$; the site condition is $V_{S30} = %d$ m/s. The UHS are taken at their mean, except for the ANCOLD `Extreme`-class closure criterion, which is taken at the $p = 0.84$ quantile. When the `Extreme`-class criterion invokes the MCE, the MCE (mean) and MCE (84%% quantile) series are added. Site `%s`.
