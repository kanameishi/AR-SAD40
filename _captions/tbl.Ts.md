Fundamental periods in [s] and shear moduli in [MPa] for a slope of %d m height, for material classifications (USCS) representative of tailings storage facilities (TSF) and waste rock dumps (WRD). `IDm` identifies the material scenario, `IDg` the geometric scenario, and `USCS` the classification of the slope materials. $H_s$ is the slope height and $b$ the geometric parameter, both in m; $s$ is the slope ratio ($\tan\beta=1/s$) and $\lambda_o$ the truncation ratio, both dimensionless; $G_o$ is the shear modulus of the profile at $z/H_s=1$, in MPa, and $m_o$ the exponent of its power law, dimensionless; $a_1$ is the first modal root, dimensionless; $V_S^o$ is the basal shear-wave velocity, in m/s; and $T_s$ is the fundamental period, in seconds.
