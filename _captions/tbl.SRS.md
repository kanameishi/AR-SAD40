Selected ground motions for site `%s`. $\mathrm{ID}_i$ identifies the record and the `Event` column the originating earthquake; $M_w$ is the moment magnitude, dimensionless; $R_{epi}$ is the epicentral distance, in km; $\mathrm{PGA}$ is the peak ground acceleration, in $g$; $I_A$ is the Arias intensity, $\mathrm{CAV}$ the cumulative absolute velocity and $\mathrm{CAV}_5$ the thresholded cumulative absolute velocity, the three in mm/s; and $c_i$ is the scale coefficient applied to each record of the suite after modal reconstruction and energy normalization, dimensionless. The total change relative to the original recording combines those three operations. Intensity measures are reported for the H1 component of each record, on which the spectral matching and the verification are performed.
