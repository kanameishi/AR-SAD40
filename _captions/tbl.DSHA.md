Probabilistic-deterministic comparison at the site condition $V_{S30}$ = %.0f m/s: maximum horizontal spectral acceleration $S_a(T_n)$ [%s] with 5%% critical damping. Rows are the oscillator period $T_n$, in seconds, with $T_n=0$ representing $\mathrm{PGA}$. The column headed $\mathrm{UHS}$ holds the mean uniform hazard spectrum of the $T_R$ = 10,000 yr return period. Each deterministic scenario $e$ contributes one column containing the conditional mean $E[S_a\mid e]$. The `MCE` and `MCE (84%%)` columns hold, respectively, the envelope of those conditional means and the envelope of the conditional quantiles $Q(0.84\mid e)$ over the set of scenarios. All ordinates are in the units stated. Site `%s`.
