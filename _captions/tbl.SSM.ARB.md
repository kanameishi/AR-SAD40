Summary inventory of the adopted ARB implementation, grouped by tectonic region type (TRT) and source type. `TRT` uses ASC for Active Shallow Crust, SCC for Stable Continental Crust, SIF for Subduction Interface, and SIS for Intraslab and Deep Seismicity. `Comp.` identifies the TRT–source-type combinations in tectonic order: C1, area sources in active shallow crust; C2, multi-point sources in active shallow crust; C3, simple fault sources in active shallow crust; C4, area sources in stable continental crust; C5, multi-point sources in stable continental crust; C6, complex fault sources of the subduction interface; and C7, area sources of intraslab and deep seismicity. Column $N$ gives the number of distinct sources in the inventory for each combination; the total of 104 corresponds to the union of the twelve source-model branches. `MFD` identifies MFD1, a truncated Gutenberg–Richter distribution (`TruncatedGRMFD`), and MFD2, a sequence of MFDs of the same type associated with the nodes of a multi-point source (`MultiMFD`). Columns $M_w$ and $z$ give the minimum–maximum ranges of moment magnitude and depth, respectively, with depth in kilometers; $M_w$ spans the maximum-magnitude perturbations of the source-model tree, and $z$ reports the extrema of the upper and lower seismogenic depths. — The inventory retains no depth coordinates for complex fault sources; their geometry is defined by the three-dimensional edges of the source files.
