Site amplification factors $\mathrm{AF}$, dimensionless, of the deterministic MCE envelope relative to the 760 m/s reference rock condition, as a function of the oscillator period $T_n$, in seconds, whose value $T_n=0$ is labelled $\mathrm{PGA}$. Each series corresponds to one probability level $p$: the `mean` level is the mean of the amplification ratio and the numerical levels are its quantiles $Q(p)$, whose extremes delimit the total variability. $V_{S30} = %s$ m/s.%s Site `%s`.
