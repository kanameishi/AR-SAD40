Earthquake recurrence and maximum-magnitude parameters of the 108 IND area sources, grouped by tectonic region type (TRT: ASC, SCC, SIF, SIS). All sources use a truncated Gutenberg–Richter distribution (truncGR) parameterised by the activity level $a$, the slope $b$, and the magnitude bounds $M_{\min}$ and $M_{\max}$; magnitudes are moment magnitudes. Columns $M_{\max}$, Low and High give the base range and the epistemic bounds under the $-0.3$ and $+0.3$ perturbations of logic-tree branch set 2. $N$ is the number of area sources per TRT.
