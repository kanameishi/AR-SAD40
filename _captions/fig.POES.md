One curve is drawn for each seismic source whose exceedance probability at the lowest $S_a(T_n)$ level reaches the display threshold and, in any case, for the five sources of highest probability; the `Total` curve is obtained from the sum of the annual exceedance rates of all sources, including those not shown individually.
