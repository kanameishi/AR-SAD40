Polar diagrams of the circumferential normal force $N_\theta$, bending moment $M_\theta$ and shear force $Q_\theta$ for the declared interface cases. Graphical amplification changes only the displayed radial length; each panel uses its own radial scale and interactive values remain in kN/m and kN·m/m.
