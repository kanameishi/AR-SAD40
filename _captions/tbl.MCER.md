ASCE/SEI 7-22 seismic design parameters for site `%s`, by site condition, obtained from the risk-targeted MCER spectrum. The spectral ordinates correspond to the mixture of the two site-demand estimates. Each row is a site condition and $V_{S30}$ is its shear-wave velocity, in m/s. $S_{aM}(0)$ is the MCER ordinate at zero period; $S_{MS}$ and $S_{M1}$ are the MCER acceleration parameters; $S_{DS}$ and $S_{D1}$ are the design acceleration parameters; these four ordinates and $S_{aM}(0)$ are in $g$. $T_0=0.2\,S_{D1}/S_{DS}$ and $T_S=S_{D1}/S_{DS}$ are the corner periods of the design spectrum, in seconds.
