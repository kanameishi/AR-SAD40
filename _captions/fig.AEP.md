Seismic hazard curves: mean annual exceedance rate (AEP), in 1/yr, of the maximum horizontal spectral acceleration $S_a(T_n)$, in $g$, with 5%% critical damping. Each curve corresponds to one oscillator period $T_n$ between %g and %g s, with $T_n=0$ corresponding to $\mathrm{PGA}$, and is identified in the legend by its $T_n$, the site (`siteID`, when more than one is shown), the $V_{S30}$ condition and the `ID` of the hazard model. The abscissa is the $S_a(T_n)$ level whose exceedance is evaluated; the secondary axis expresses the same ordinate as the return period $T_R$, the inverse of the rate, in years. The mean of the ground-motion model set is plotted, and the curves extend down to a rate of $10^{-6}$ 1/yr. Site condition $V_{S30} = %s$ m/s. Site `%s`.
