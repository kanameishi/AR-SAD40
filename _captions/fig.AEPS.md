Seismic hazard curves by source for the oscillator period $T_n = %g$ s, with $T_n=0$ corresponding to $\mathrm{PGA}$: mean annual exceedance rate (AEP), in 1/yr, of the maximum horizontal spectral acceleration $S_a(T_n)$, in $g$, with 5%% critical damping. One curve is drawn for each seismic source of the source model, together with the `Total` curve, the sum of the rates of all sources at each $S_a(T_n)$ level. The abscissa is the $S_a(T_n)$ level whose exceedance is evaluated; the secondary axis expresses the same ordinate as the return period $T_R$, the inverse of the rate, in years. The mean of the ground-motion model set is plotted, and the curves extend down to a rate of $10^{-6}$ 1/yr. Site condition $V_{S30} = %s$ m/s. Site `%s`.
