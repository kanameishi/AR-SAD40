Earthquake recurrence and maximum-magnitude parameters of the 255 XAF sources by source group: 115 simple fault sources with incremental MFDs (occurrence rates specified per 0.1-magnitude bin from $M_{\min} = 6.05$), 86 multi-point sources with truncated Gutenberg–Richter MFDs (MPS/SC), and 54 background multi-point sources with arbitrary MFDs (BG). The columns give the ranges of $a$, $b$, $M_{\min}$, and $M_{\max}$ in moment magnitude; a dash indicates that the parameter does not apply to the MFD type. $N$ is the number of sources per group.
