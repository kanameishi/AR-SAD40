ASCE/SEI 7-22 seismic design parameters obtained from the risk-targeted MCER spectrum. Each row identifies a site (`siteID`) and a site condition; $V_{S30}$ is its shear-wave velocity, in m/s. $S_{aM}(0)$ is the MCER ordinate at zero period; $S_{MS}$ and $S_{M1}$ are the MCER acceleration parameters; $S_{DS}$ and $S_{D1}$ are the design acceleration parameters; these four ordinates and $S_{aM}(0)$ are in %s. $T_0$ and $T_S$ are the corner periods of the design spectrum, in seconds.
