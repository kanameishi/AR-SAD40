Site-specific seismic demand spectra for maximum horizontal spectral accelerations $S_a(T_n)$, in $g$, with 5%% critical damping. The curves are the risk-targeted ASCE/SEI 7-22 MCER spectrum, the design spectrum —defined as two thirds of the MCER— and the code-form design spectrum of Section 11.4.5, built piecewise from $S_{DS}$ and $S_{D1}$; with no long-period transition $T_L$ adopted outside the U.S. maps, the $S_{D1}/T_n$ branch of the latter extends to the end of the grid. $V_{S30}=%d$ m/s.
