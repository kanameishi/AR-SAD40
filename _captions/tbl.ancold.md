ANCOLD Seismic Design Criteria by Consequence Category. Source: ANCOLD Guidelines on Tailings Dams [-@ancold2019]. ANCOLD defines six consequence categories based on population at risk (PAR) and expected loss of life, with three gradations for the High category: High C, High B, and High A. OBE and SEE are assigned by consequence category; for post-closure, the SEE of the applicable category is adopted and is reassessed at closure upon changes in the hazard, the condition of the dam, or its consequences. For the Extreme category, the greater of the MCE and the 1/10,000 AEP event governs, evaluated at the 84th percentile. PAR = population at risk.
