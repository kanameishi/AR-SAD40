Deterministic scenarios for site `%s`, as declared in the calculation specifications. $e$ is the scenario identifier and TRT the tectonic region type that selects its ground-motion logic tree. $M_w$ is the moment magnitude of the rupture. $R_{epi}$ is the epicentral distance (km), the median over the rupture variants. Depth is the hypocentral depth (km), reported as the min–max range across the declared rupture variants. Strike, Dip, and Rake are the rupture-plane angles (°); Rake lists the discrete values declared across variants. MSR is the magnitude scaling relation that sizes the rupture, coded as AH = AllenHayesInterfaceBilinear, L14 = Leonard2014_SCR, STF = StrasserInterface, STS = StrasserIntraslab, THR = ThingbaijamReverseFault, WC94 = WC1994. Aspect is the rupture length-to-width aspect ratio (min–max range across variants). Variants is the number of declared rupture variants of the scenario.
