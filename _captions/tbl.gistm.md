GISTM Seismic Design Criteria by Consequence Class. Source: GISTM [-@gistm2020]. The Global Industry Standard on Tailings Management (GISTM) defines five consequence classes for tailings storage facilities, based on population at risk (PAR) and the severity of environmental and socio-economic impacts. For all classes, the maximum credible earthquake (MCE) governs if it produces more severe ground motions than the probabilistic criterion. Post-closure criteria are uniform across all classes. GISTM does not use OBE/SEE terminology or dual-level design earthquakes.
