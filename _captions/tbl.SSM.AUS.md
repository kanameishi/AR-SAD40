Inventory of source identities in the adopted AUS implementation, consolidated by `providerID` across the 16 model branches. `TRT` uses ASC for active or non-cratonic shallow crust, SCC for stable or cratonic continental crust, SIF for subduction interface, and SIS for intraslab seismicity; ASC/SCC indicates that the assignment changes between branches. Columns `Area`, `Point`, `SimpleFault`, and `ComplexFault` identify area, point, simple-fault, and complex-fault sources. The total of 394,003 comprises 335 area sources, 393,240 point sources, 418 simple faults, and 10 complex faults.
