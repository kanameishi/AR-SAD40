Seismogenic depth parameters of the XAF sources by source type, in kilometres. `z_upper` and `z_lower` are the upper and lower seismogenic depths. For multi-point sources, the last column gives the discrete hypocentral depth distribution — depth with its weight in parentheses — for the WAF sources (MPS-1 to MPS-6) and the mean-depth range for the collapsed SSA sources; fault-source ruptures derive their geometry from the fault model.
