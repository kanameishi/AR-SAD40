## Scope of service

- Review of available seismic hazard studies and identification of the most up-to-date source models;
- Review and adoption of the state-of-the-art regional seismic source model (SSM) applicable to the sites;
- Probabilistic Seismic Hazard Analysis (PSHA) and seismic hazard disaggregation for the [N] project sites;
- Deterministic Seismic Hazard Analysis (DSHA) of the Maximum Credible Earthquake (MCE) scenarios;
- Site response analysis based on ergodic models;
- Selection and spectral adjustment of ground-motion records for the dynamic analyses;
- Seismic design criteria for TSF, WRD, pit slopes, conventional structures, and tanks and liquid containment structures (optional).

Formal review of previous studies or discussion of differences found are not included in the scope.
