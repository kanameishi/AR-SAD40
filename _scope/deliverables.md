## Deliverables

At the end of the work, the following deliverables will be presented:

- *Seismic hazard assessment and seismic design criteria report:* seismic source model, PSHA and DSHA results for the [N] sites, site response analysis, seismic hazard disaggregation, design earthquakes, and the seismic design criteria for the evaluated facilities. Format: HTML report.
- *Executive summary:* executive summary memorandum of the seismic hazard assessment and the seismic design criteria. Format: DOCX.
- *Findings presentation:* presentation of the seismic hazard results, the design ground motions, and the seismic design criteria. Format: HTML presentation.
- *Ground motion records:* the acceleration, velocity, and displacement time histories for the dynamic analyses, with their response spectra, intensity measures, significant durations, scale factors, and event and station provenance. Format: digital data files.
