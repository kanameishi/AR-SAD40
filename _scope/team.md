## Team

The service will be carried out by the following professionals from SRK Consulting:

Alejandro Verri Kozlowski is a Professional Civil Engineer and Corporate Consultant at SRK Consulting (Argentina). His practice is focused on reliability- and risk-informed engineering under uncertainty, integrating geotechnical earthquake engineering, probabilistic seismic hazard assessment, numerical modelling, and scientific programming for mining and civil infrastructure.

Over thirty years of practice — as consultant and director of Verri & Sfriso until its merger with SRK, and at SRK Consulting since 2008 — he has developed site-specific seismic hazard assessments, design ground motions, and performance-based seismic design criteria for projects across Africa, Asia, Australia, and the Americas. His seismic design experience covers tailings storage facilities and waste rock dumps, embankment and rockfill dams, tunnels and hydraulic plugs, buried pipelines, tanks, and pile-supported wharves, and includes the peer review of seismic hazard studies and seismic design criteria.

He is the author of engineering software libraries for seismic hazard assessment, ground-motion processing, and dynamic site and slope response, including R packages published on CRAN. He has held professorships at the University of Buenos Aires and the Torcuato Di Tella University, is author or co-author of sixteen technical publications, has received the Antonio Marín Award of the National Academy of Engineering for contributions to civil engineering in Argentina, and has delivered keynote lectures on the seismic design of tailings and rockfill dams.
